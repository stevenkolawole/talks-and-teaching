# Building a Sign-to-Speech Prototype

TensorFlow, PyTorch, and DeepStack — and what it takes to make it work in sub-Saharan Africa.

Masakhane Speaker Series; IJCAI 2022 AI-for-Social-Good demo; Deep Learning Indaba 2022
