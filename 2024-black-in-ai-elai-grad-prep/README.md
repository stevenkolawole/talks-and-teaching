# Black in AI — Emerging Leaders in AI (ELAI) Grad-Prep Panels

Two invited panel appearances on the [Black in AI](https://blackinai.org/) Emerging Leaders in AI
grad-prep programme, on getting into graduate school and on what to do when an application does not
land. Both sessions ran as interactive Q&A, so there are no slides.

## Final Push to Submit (2024)

On applying to graduate school — what I did, what I would do differently, and how to submit a
compelling application. The organisers reported that attendees left with more clarity and, in many
cases, less impostor syndrome.

## What's Next After an Unsuccessful Application? (2025)

On regrouping after a rejection: what an unsuccessful round actually tells you and what to change
before the next one. Run twice, on 18 and 19 March 2025, to cover both time zones.

**Venue:** Black in AI, Emerging Leaders in AI — Grad Prep *(invited panellist)*
**Materials:** none — both panels were unscripted Q&A with no slides.

See also my statement of purpose, published publicly at [cs-sop.org](https://cs-sop.org/), and the
[CMU Africa SOP/CV talk](../2025-cmu-africa-research-club/).
